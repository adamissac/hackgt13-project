# Audio cue sheet

The score is original and generated by `scripts/music.py` (run `npm run prepare:assets`). Tempos are picked so one beat is a
whole number of frames, and the scenes put cuts, captions and taps on that grid:

- Main cut: 120 BPM, 15 frames per beat, a bar every 2 seconds.
- Vertical cut: the same edit at 1.25x, so 150 BPM, 12 frames per beat.

Section boundaries live in the `music` block of `src/timeline.json` (frames).

## Structure (main cut)

| Time | Frames | Section |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:08 | 0 to 240 | Intro: low drone, ticking eighths, a soft heartbeat kick, snare roll and riser into the drop |
| 0:08 | 240 | Drop: impact and crash as the Constellation wordmark lands; full groove starts |
| 0:08 to 0:50 | 240 to 1500 | Groove: four-on-the-floor kick, claps on 2 and 4, 16th hats, pumping bass, Dmaj9 / Bm7 / Gmaj7 / A6sus pads, the bell hook every 8 bars |
| 0:12, 0:22, 0:32, 0:40, 0:50 | 360, 660, 960, 1200, 1500 | Snare fill and crash into every scene change |
| 0:50 to 0:58 | 1500 to 1740 | Montage peak: 16th arpeggio, open hats, shaker; roll and riser into the Constellation |
| 0:58 to 1:06 | 1740 to 1980 | Break: drums drop out, wide pads and star bells; the last bar builds back in |
| 1:06 to 1:10 | 1980 to 2100 | Tech beat: groove returns with a fast arpeggio, roll into the ending |
| 1:10 | 2100 | Final hit and Dmaj9 chord as the lockup lands, ringing out to 1:15 |

The vertical cut follows the same plan: drop at 192, fills at 288, 528 and 720, break at 960, final hit at 1152.

## Sound effects

Every UI sound is its own `<Audio>` layer (`src/components/AudioLayer.tsx`), placed on the same beat as the thing it
belongs to. Mute them with `--props='{"sfx":false}'`, or swap the bed with `--props='{"musicFile":"music.mp3"}'`.
